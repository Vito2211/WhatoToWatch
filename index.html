How Shared Links Work

When someone shares a movie or collection from What to Watch?, the data is compressed into a compact, self-contained payload. There is no central server holding your data — it travels directly between you and the sender.

If you don't have the What to Watch? app installed yet, you can preview the contents of your link below or install the app to import it into your personal library.

Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent details on architecture, data privacy, and upcoming releases.

Why doesn't the website automatically open the Android app yet?

Android App Links require domain ownership verification via a digital asset file (assetlinks.json) hosted on a production HTTPS domain. GitHub Pages project sites (github.io) do not automatically enable universal app links without specific root domain configuration. Direct in-app importing works via the whattowatch:// deep link scheme once installed.

Where is my movie data stored?

100% locally on your Android device using SQLite and Room. What to Watch? has no remote database, no user accounts, and no background tracking. Your data is yours alone.

Can I backup or transfer my watchlists?

Yes. The app includes built-in JSON backup and restore capabilities, as well as exportable .whattowatch files that can be sent over any messenger, email, or drive.

When will the app be available on Google Play?

The app is currently in preparation for release. You can monitor the open-source repository on GitHub for APK releases and announcement of the Google Play Store listing.
